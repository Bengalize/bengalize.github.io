Musa, a Tailor in Bamako, Gets Found on Google

A 51-second animated video, written and voiced entirely in Bambara — a language with almost no business content online. Musa's tailoring shop sits empty until he puts it on Google; then the customers come. I wrote and voiced it to show local business owners in Mali, in their own language, what being findable actually does.

Why Bambara

A language the internet skips

Business advice online is overwhelmingly in English and French. The shop owners who would benefit most from a Google listing in Bamako largely work in Bambara — and almost nobody makes business content for them in it. This video is a small proof that the gap is fillable: one story, one tailor, one clear idea, in the language of the people it is for.

It is also the seed of the agency work collected at Artizanale — Bellevue Web & Ads: the same lesson, taught on two continents.
