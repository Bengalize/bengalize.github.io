Student project · Harrisburg University · 2024

AI-Powered Career Coach for Job Applications & Networking

As a student at Harrisburg University, I built an AI assistant to help students optimize résumés for applicant tracking systems, draft tailored cover letters, and practice interviews — with links to university networking events and career-center booking.

About Bengaly Cisse
BotpressChatbotHarrisburg University2024
What it was built to do

One assistant for the whole application run

Job applications break into the same few tasks for every student. The coach put them in one conversation.

Résumés

Optimized for applicant tracking systems

Students could check a résumé against how applicant tracking systems (ATS) read it, and get guidance on bringing it up to standard before applying.

Cover letters

Drafted, tailored letters

The assistant was built to draft cover letters tailored to the role a student was applying for, instead of one generic letter sent everywhere.

Interviews

Practice before the real thing

Students could practice interviews with the assistant ahead of the real conversation.

Networking

Events and the career center

The coach also pointed students to university networking events and to booking the career center — the human help, one link away from the automated help.

The build

Built on Botpress, as a student project

The career coach was a student project at Harrisburg University in 2024, built on Botpress, a chatbot platform. It is the earliest AI build in this portfolio — made while I was still an undergraduate, before the production data work at Amazon.

The coach ran as a hosted Botpress webchat while the project was active; it is no longer online. This page is the record of the build.
